Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

GTA6 Autopilot is a personal tool operated by its owner. It publishes short video clips that the owner has made to the owner's own social media accounts: the YouTube channels "GTA 6 på Svenska" and "The GTA 6 Feed" and the owner's Instagram professional accounts. It has no other users.

Data we access

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the authorized channel's name and ID, and the ability to upload videos the owner has created to that channel.
  • Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login): the authorized account's username and ID, its publishing quota, and the ability to publish Reels the owner has created.

No data about any other person is collected, and no viewer, follower or commenter data is read or stored.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and on the owner's own computer, and are used only by the owner's scheduled job to call Google's and Meta's APIs. Logs of published clips (titles and public links) are kept in that private repository. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and are deleted when it is retired. Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions or via Google security settings; Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.

Third-party terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of Instagram is subject to Instagram's Terms of Use.
